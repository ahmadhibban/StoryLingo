"Mention in the Book (Quran) Idris (Enoch). Verily! He was a man of truth (and) a prophet. We raised him to a high station."

This section illustrates the two key phases of Prophet Idris's mission: his initial call in Babylon and his subsequent migration and establishment of a community in Egypt. This journey showcases his resilience and the evolution of his teachings.

Born as the fifth generation descendant of Prophet Adam, Idris was raised in Babylon upon the original teachings of his forefathers. His mission began here, calling people back to the true faith.

His message was met with resistance. Only a few listened, while the majority turned away, creating a great challenge for the nascent community.

In Egypt, Prophet Idris and his followers established a new community. He continued his mission, calling people to what is just and fair and providing a framework for a righteous society.

🙏 Prayers: Taught specific prayers to foster spiritual connection.

🌙 Fasting: Instructed fasting on certain days for purification.

🤝 Charity: Commanded giving a portion of wealth to the poor.

Prophet Idris's legacy is defined by more than his spiritual guidance. This section explores his unique status and a monumental innovation that forever changed human civilization, showcasing the link between divine guidance and societal advancement.

He is credited as the first person to invent the basic form of writing. This revolutionary contribution allowed for the preservation and dissemination of knowledge, laws, and divine revelations for all future generations.

He was the first of the Children of Adam to be granted prophethood after Adam and Seth. This places him in a pivotal role, continuing the chain of divine guidance in the early history of humanity.
